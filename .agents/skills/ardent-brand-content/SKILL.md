# ARDENT BRAND CONTENT SKILL

Write content that makes Ardent recognizable and helps a customer understand the watch they are considering. Credibility and product clarity take priority over grand claims.

## Sources and scope

Read the latest available Ardent Brand Bible, Product Truth, Website Vision, and Design System before writing substantial copy. Check current Shopify product data and approved service policies for live commercial facts. A newer explicit decision from Sacha takes priority over an older draft.

Use `ardent-media` for image, video, and 3D creation; `ardent-ui-ux` for interface behavior; and `ardent-shopify` for publishing copy through theme or store data. This skill governs the message, voice, structure, translation, and factual review.

If a source is missing or conflicts with another, keep unsupported facts out of customer copy and identify what must be verified. Do not make a plausible sentence sound like a confirmed product fact.

## Brand voice

Ardent is ambitious, premium, young, passionate, French, modern, precise, and elegant. Express those qualities without pretension, clichés, or imitation of an established watch house.

- Write with a sober, assured, precise, elegant, human tone.
- Prefer concrete words about the watch, materials, choices, assembly, details, and experience.
- Let important sentences be short; leave room for the visual work to speak.
- Make the next customer action clear without pressure or artificial urgency.
- Avoid generic luxury language, constant superlatives, dropshipping language, exaggerated exclamation, and unverifiable uniqueness claims.
- Present the founders' real story and the brand's youth honestly when relevant. Do not invent heritage, manufacture status, or a connection to another house.

## Product and service truth

- Verify every claim about origin, materials, movement, dimensions, resistance, water resistance, accuracy, power reserve, finish, stock, price, assembly, delivery, returns, warranty, and care before publication.
- Use the current Product Truth and store records, not historical supplier prices or old conversation estimates.
- Explain customization in terms of choices the customer can actually make. Do not promise an option or combination that the configurator cannot supply.
- Distinguish **assembled by Ardent** from claims such as **made in France**. Use each only when its precise meaning is supported.
- If a detail is uncertain, request the fact or write around it; do not fill the gap with a confident guess.
- Service pages should answer practical questions directly: what will arrive, how it is assembled, timing, delivery, returns, warranty, maintenance, and contact, using current policies.

## Collection storytelling

Give each collection its own emotional setting while keeping one Ardent voice:

- **Méridian — fire:** energy, intensity, matter, controlled heat.
- **Bastille — water:** depth, movement, composure.
- **Élysée — air:** elevation, lightness, elegance.

Use the element to support the watch, not to replace product information with fantasy. The collection name is a brand identity, not evidence of a technical specification.

## Content by surface

- **Homepage and campaign:** one clear idea per visual moment; concise text and a meaningful next action.
- **Collection page:** establish the universe, show the range, then lead naturally to a real product or configurator.
- **Product page:** name the watch, describe verified options and distinguishing details, explain the purchase and service terms plainly.
- **Configurator:** short option names, helpful differences, honest availability messages, and a readable configuration summary.
- **Email and social drafts:** adapt the same voice to the channel; do not create false scarcity or pressure.

Do not publish or send a draft merely because it has been written. Follow the current request's scope for any external action.

## French and English

Write naturally in each language rather than translating word for word. Preserve the official collection names and factual meaning. Keep product claims, option names, prices, service terms, and calls to action aligned across FR and EN.

In French, use the brand's established customer address and terminology when available. Check accents and the official spelling **Méridian**. Review English copy for its own rhythm and clarity. Fit both languages into the actual interface, especially on mobile.

## Final review

Before delivery, check: Is every factual claim sourced? Does the copy sound like Ardent? Can a new customer tell what is sold, what can be personalized, and what happens next? Is the French identity subtle? Are FR and EN consistent? Does the wording remain clear beside the actual visuals?

Report any unresolved factual or policy question separately from the finished copy. Never hide an **à vérifier** item inside polished customer-facing text.
